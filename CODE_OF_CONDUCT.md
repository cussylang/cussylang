# Code of conduct

Cussy welcomes contributors of all experience levels and backgrounds. Treat
people with respect in issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces.

- Discuss the code and ideas without personal attacks.
- Give constructive feedback and assume good faith when resolving mistakes.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish private information without permission.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or post sexualized abuse.
- Keep jokes and project slang welcoming; they do not excuse mistreating others.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, lock discussions, and
restrict participation when necessary. They should consider context, explain
decisions when appropriate, and apply these standards consistently.

To report a problem, contact a repository maintainer through a contact method
they have made public. If none is available, open a minimal issue requesting a
private contact channel without naming the affected person or sharing private
details. Reports should be handled discreetly, with information shared only as
needed to respond.

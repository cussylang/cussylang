# Channel research and naming provenance

Collected **October 3, 2026 (America/Chicago)** from the publicly accessible
Videos and Live pages of [whitecap / @whitecaplol](https://www.youtube.com/@whitecaplol)
and [The Desmos Guy / @desmos-guy](https://www.youtube.com/@desmos-guy).

The channel pages were read directly after the search browser could not render
them. The public page metadata exposed channel identities, descriptions, initial
video cards, and stream cards. Two individual video descriptions were also read.
The compact [metadata snapshot](../research/channel-snapshot.json) records URLs
and the accessible cards; it is a dated sample, not an exhaustive channel archive.

## Verified Whitecap themes

The channel description identifies Desmos graphing and Geometry Dash as its focus.
Video cards repeatedly feature Desmos audio covers and GD-related animation.
The public [Limbo keys expansion video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nkenSjQVrJs)
explains adapting the key shuffle beyond the original grid and describes the
mathematical work involved. This supports the key-count, loop, and graph-engine
flavor used in Cussy lore; no claim is made that the creator used our invented words.

Other directly observed video cards include
[iSpyWithMyGraphingCalculator](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MB6mEbzbh8A),
[Thinking Space II in Desmos](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X7Xz-Y0GQI4),
[the Heliopolis audio cover](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iTJkaGtDGKU), and
[a Tidal Wave Desmos performance](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bP6r5PQHpfY).
These informed the larger demo's playful name and the repeated graph/audio/demon
vocabulary. Cussy itself does not implement an audio synthesizer.

The [Live page](https://www.youtube.com/@whitecaplol/streams) exposes a numbered
GD Colon graphing series and a
[Monopoly collaboration with The Desmos Guy](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FqBwwHknYoI).
Those support the recurring project-day and collaboration themes. They do not
establish the supplied stream or papa phrases as actual repeated chat catchphrases.

## Verified The Desmos Guy themes

The channel description and keywords identify Desmos, graphing calculators, math,
and Geometry Dash. The
[Heliopolis completion video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o54AnShNdEk)
describes completing a project over 100 days and thanks supporters, including
Whitecap. The [Live page](https://www.youtube.com/@desmos-guy/streams) contains
numbered days with percentage progress; this inspired the 100-step
`examples/heliopolis.cussy` simulation. It is a thematic example, not a reconstruction
of the actual level or graph.

Other observed project cards include
[Nullscapes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eu6l-f5S3q8),
[Sakupen Circles](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KAGFoDrU4Hk),
[Monopoly](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6Ln7-o1UeKw), and
[a live Monopoly collaboration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lEB7AFzDMZg).
They support using small game simulations alongside graph expressions and progress
utilities. The overlapping Monopoly streams are directly linked evidence of the
channels' collaboration, rather than an inferred relationship from similar names.

## Requested jokes versus observations

The spellings `jole` and its variants, **whitecaplol hop on stream**, and
**whitecaplol is our papa** come from the user's specification. They are mandatory
project branding here. The accessed sample does **not** independently verify that
these are creator quotations or phrases frequently repeated by either community.

Cussy-specific combinations such as papacoded, joleware, graphdemon, STREAMLESS,
AURA_OVERFLOW, yapcompiler, and “a compiler with 200 Limbo keys and one return
statement” are inventions for this project. Their inspiration is documented;
they are not represented as discovered quotes, official terminology, or endorsements.

## Coverage limits

Only initial public page results were inspected: no historical pagination,
private content, transcripts, comments, member posts, or live-chat logs. Video
availability and ordering can change after collection. References to a video's
“Top 1” title describe that title, not a verified current difficulty ranking.
All local stream state is fictional runtime state. The language is an unofficial
fan project and does not claim affiliation with the creators, Desmos, or Geometry Dash.
